# RevPilot AI — five-minute pitch

## 0:00–0:45 — The problem

Merchants have plenty of transaction data but no safe, focused way to turn
it into the next revenue action. Generic AI can suggest a discount, but it
must not be trusted with pricing, inventory, or payment execution.

## 0:45–1:30 — The product

RevPilot is an AI revenue agent for merchants. It finds cross-sell,
reactivation, and bundle opportunities, explains the evidence, simulates
the likely outcome, and acts only inside merchant-defined policies and
permissions.

## 1:30–2:30 — Show the dashboard

Open Command Center and point out that revenue, repeat-purchase rate, top
products, and opportunities are live database metrics. Open an opportunity
to show the customer reach, affinity evidence, confidence, risk, and the
explicitly labeled estimate.

## 2:30–3:30 — Show safe action

Ask the Agent to simulate a discount and create a campaign draft. Show the
approval request, policy result, and audit trace. Approve it and explain that
the server recomputes the financial action before the payment provider is
called.

## 3:30–4:15 — Show the buyer side

Open Shop with AI. Ask for a product recommendation, compare two products,
add an item to the conversational cart, preview checkout, explicitly
confirm, and complete the demo payment. Show that the order and attribution
are created from verified backend data.

## 4:15–5:00 — Show trust

Open Control Room and Failure Lab. Trigger one failure, inspect the trace,
then recover it. Finish with the architecture statement:

> AI proposes. Deterministic services verify. Merchant policy decides.
> Webhooks confirm. Attribution measures.
